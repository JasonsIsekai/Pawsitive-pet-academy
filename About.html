Company Vision

+

At Pawsitive Pet Academy we want to improve the wellbeing of pets while creating meaningful employment opportunities for individuals interested in working in the pet care industry or starting their own pet related businesses

Company Mission

+
  • To provide pet care education and parctical animal training for pets owners, aspiring pet care professionals, and animal lovers
  • To bridge the knowledge gap by equipping people with essential skills in proper pet care, training, nutrition and emergency care
  • To relfect a caringf , trustworthy and professional nature through it's educational service and academy environment

Company Goals

+

Here at Pawsitive Pet Academy we want to expand our business and to grow a digital presence through a dedicated website and mobile application to advertise the business, handle customer information requests and provide automated services quotes. We want to offer flexible learning options for individuals, families, pet sitters, staff, veterinary assistansts and aspiring entrepreneurs, encouraging multi-course enrollment through tiered discouts.

Company History

+

Pawsitive Pet Academy was founded in 2023 in Durban. Since it's inception, hundreds of pet owners and aspiring pet care professionals have successfully completed its courses, which include six-month professional development programmes and six-week practical skills courses.
